The final answer states the time zone and the UTC time used for tomorrow at 2 pm at Aspen, gives Vref and the landing distance against the available landing distance, and reports the AFM go-around climb gradient check. For the approach used (including circling approaches with no runway listed), it uses the missed-approach course converted from magnetic to true, the SF50 climb gradient along it, and a terrain comparison starting at the missed-approach point. It compares against 200 ft/NM unless a higher published gradient is known, and says the chart's gradient is not in CIFP. It treats a not_established terrain result as inconclusive, never calls the go-around safe or certified, and says the figures are for planning only.
