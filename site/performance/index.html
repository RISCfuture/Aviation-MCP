Aircraft
performance tools

Four tools calculate takeoff, landing, climb and takeoff limits from current runway data and weather. Each takes an aircraft object, and each result says which manuals its figures come from and which outputs those manuals don’t cover.

Cirrus SF50Cirrus SR22

Cirrus SF50 Vision Jet

Aircraft object {"type": "sf50", "generation": "g2_plus"}
generation
g1, g2, g2_updated_thrust (a G2 with SB5X-72-01), g2_plus or g3. The G2+, G3 and updated G2 use the Updated Thrust Schedule supplement (31452-111) over the G2 AFM.
In the AFM

Vref, the go-around climb gradient check, contaminated-runway landings (wet, water or slush, slush or wet snow, dry snow, compact snow and RwyCC), ice protection, and the enroute-obstacle climb.

Not in the AFM

Balked landing climb, time, fuel and distance to climb, and the air conditioner and wet grass corrections. Results list these under unavailable_outputs.

01 / Flaps 50%

Takeoff

Ground run and distance over a 50 ft obstacle, each correction factor, the takeoff climb gradient, and margins against TORA and TODA.

takeoff_performance
Example question
I’m flying a G2+ SF50 out of Oakland (KOAK) runway 28R in 18 hours at 5,800 lb. What will my ground run and total takeoff distance be, and what correction factors apply?
02 / Up, 50% or 100%, with ice

Landing

Vref, landing run and distance from 50 ft on a dry, wet or contaminated runway, and whether the AFM go-around climb gradient is met.

landing_performance
Example question
My G1 SF50 lands at Truckee (KTRK) runway 20 at 5,200 lb with a quarter inch of wet snow reported. What are my Vref and landing distance?
03 / Five climb schedules

Climb profile

Gradient and rate by altitude for the takeoff, enroute obstacle and enroute climbs, with and without ice protection, in the forecast winds aloft. Gives a gradient to compare with terrain.

climb_profile
Example question
Will my G2+ SF50 at 5,900 lb out-climb the terrain taking off from Aspen (KASE) runway 15 right now?
04 / Weight or temperature

Takeoff limits

The maximum takeoff weight, or the hottest temperature at a weight, for one runway end or every end of an airport, and the constraint that sets it.

takeoff_limits
Example question
What’s the hottest outside air temperature at which my G2 SF50 can still take off from Telluride (KTEX) in either direction at maximum takeoff weight?

sf50_takeoff_performance, sf50_landing_performance, sf50_climb_profile and sf50_takeoff_limits are deprecated aliases that take aircraft_generation instead of an aircraft object. They will be removed.

Cirrus SR22

Aircraft object {"type": "sr22", "hartzell_propeller": false, "turbonormalized": true}
hartzell_propeller
The Hartzell composite propeller (POH supplement 13772-129). Its enroute climb tables replace the POH’s.
turbonormalized
The Tornado Alley turbonormalizing system (STC SA10588SC). It raises the ceiling to 25,000 ft and adds lean-of-peak climb figures. Takeoff stays limited to 10,000 ft.

Both options are required. The figures are for the pre-G3 wing POH (13772-001), and results carry the supplements’ notes, including that no document covers both supplements together.

In the POH

Balked landing climb, time, fuel and distance to climb (lean of peak too when turbonormalized), the wet grass correction for takeoff and landing, and the air conditioner correction for takeoff.

Not in the POH

Vref, the go-around gradient check, contaminated-runway data other than wet grass, ice protection, and the enroute-obstacle climb. Results list these under unavailable_outputs, and contamination other than wet grass is refused.

01 / Flaps 50%

Takeoff

Ground roll and distance over a 50 ft obstacle, with the slope, grass, wet grass and air conditioner corrections. Above 10,000 ft the POH does not authorize a takeoff.

takeoff_performance
Example question
The grass at Boulder (KBDU) is wet after this morning’s rain. How much of runway 26G will my SR22 use taking off at 3,300 lb?
02 / Flaps 100%

Landing

Landing roll and distance from 50 ft, with the wind, slope, grass and wet grass corrections, and the balked landing climb gradient and rate.

landing_performance
Example question
I’m landing my turbonormalized SR22 with the Hartzell composite prop on Boulder’s (KBDU) wet grass runway 08G at 3,300 lb. What’s my landing distance, and what can I expect from a balked landing climb?
03 / Takeoff and enroute

Climb profile

Gradient and rate by altitude in the forecast winds aloft, with the time, fuel and distance to climb. These leave out the POH’s start, taxi and takeoff fuel and its hot-day addition, and say so.

climb_profile
Example question
How long will it take, and how much fuel, to climb my Hartzell-equipped SR22 from Reno (KRNO) runway 17R to 12,500 ft at 3,400 lb?
04 / Weight or temperature

Takeoff limits

The maximum takeoff weight, or the hottest temperature at a weight, for one runway end or every end of an airport, including a wet grass strip.

takeoff_limits
Example question
What’s the most my turbonormalized SR22 can weigh to take off from Leadville (KLXV) on a 25 °C afternoon, and what limits it on each runway?

Figures come from digitized manual tables, or from regression equations fitted to them that extrapolate with an uncertainty. Anything the tables don’t cover is reported, not estimated.

For pre-flight planning only, not for cockpit use. These figures are not approved by the FAA or by Cirrus Aircraft; verify them with the aircraft’s flight manual.
